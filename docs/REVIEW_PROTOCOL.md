# A small independent review

This protocol is ready to use; no sessions or participants have been recorded yet. Public examples only. Do not upload internal documents or personal data.

## Search, about five minutes

1. Paste two short public or invented documents with a fact present in only one.
2. Ask for that fact in different words. Find and open the source.
3. Ask for a related fact that neither document contains.
4. Explain what the score and returned passage mean in your own words.

Record task completion, wrong-source selections, whether the person mistakes retrieval for confirmation, and the step where they hesitate. Ask what they expected before explaining the interface. Keep the exact public query so someone else can reproduce the result.

## Task review, about five minutes

1. Add a task with a deliverable and owner, then select a date.
2. Find why the first sample task needs attention.
3. Delete a task and undo the deletion.
4. Export the list and identify whether the tool has approved it.

Record completion, assistance, misinterpretation of advisory flags and whether the person understands that unsaved data disappears on close.
