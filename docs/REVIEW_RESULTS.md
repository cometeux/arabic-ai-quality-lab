## Findings and changes

No observations are entered until a real review has occurred.

For each future finding, record: date and tested build; public issue link; task; observation; completed/assisted/abandoned; effect; change or reason for deferral; linked commit; retest date, build and result. Preserve failed and unresolved observations.

A retest is recorded separately from the original session. Repeated attempts by one reviewer must not be presented as additional independent people. Model-generated feedback and maintainer checks must be labeled separately.

## What would support a stronger claim?

- A working-feature claim: a reproducible functional check with its environment.
- A task-success claim: observed completion criteria and actual session counts, including assistance and failures.
- An improvement claim: a comparable before/after task and the limits of the sample.
- An adoption claim: actual, attributable usage evidence collected with appropriate permission.

These are evidence requirements, not completed milestones.
