# Make the work yours through decisions and evidence

These are new AI-assisted experiments, not proof of independent programming proficiency. Before using them in an application:

1. Run both demos. Explain why “اجازة” behaves differently from “إجازة” in the raw baseline.
2. Inspect the unsupported five-day remote-work example. Explain why word overlap is not evidence that a claim is true.
3. Add five queries without editing the ranker. Label expected source IDs yourself, including one unsupported query. Record what failed.
4. Change one normalization rule and predict its effect before running the evaluation. Keep the result, even if worse.
5. In Action Review, try a valid task containing “متابعة”. Explain a false positive and decide whether to keep, narrow or remove that rule.
6. Explain the required/advisory distinction, date validation, and why ownership is not inferred automatically.
7. Commit your fixture change and a short note describing your decision and result. Do not backdate commits or claim pre-existing work.

The current release is a small offline prototype with synthetic fixtures; I am validating it through specific cases.” Adjust that wording only after doing and understanding the work.
